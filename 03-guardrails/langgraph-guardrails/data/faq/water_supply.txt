Water Supply Complaints

- To report no water supply or a burst pipeline, call the ward helpline or use the "Water Complaint" option in the municipal app.
- Tanker requests are free for areas with no piped connection; apply 24 hours in advance through the ward office.
- Billing disputes must be raised within 30 days of the bill date, with the last paid receipt attached.
- The municipal corporation will NEVER call you to ask for your bank OTP, UPI PIN, or account number to "process a refund" — this is a scam. Water bill refunds are only issued as credit on the next bill.
