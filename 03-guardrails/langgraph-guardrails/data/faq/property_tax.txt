Property Tax

- Check your outstanding property tax on the municipal website using your Property ID (PID), printed on your last tax receipt.
- Early payment (before the financial year starts) gets a 5% rebate for residential properties.
- Late payment after the due date attracts a 2% monthly penalty.
- Pay online via the official municipal portal only. The corporation will never ask you to pay property tax through a personal UPI ID or WhatsApp link.
