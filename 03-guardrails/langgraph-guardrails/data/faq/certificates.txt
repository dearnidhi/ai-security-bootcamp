Birth and Death Certificates

- Birth certificate: apply within 21 days of birth for free processing; after 21 days, a late fee applies and an affidavit is required.
- Death certificate: apply within 21 days with the hospital's death summary or, for home deaths, a doctor's certificate.
- Documents needed: hospital discharge summary, parents' ID proof (for birth), applicant's ID proof.
- Processing time is 7 working days; certificates are collected in person from the ward office with an original ID proof.
