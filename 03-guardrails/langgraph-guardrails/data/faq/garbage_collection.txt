Garbage Collection

- Door-to-door collection runs 7 AM to 10 AM daily; segregate wet and dry waste into separate bins.
- Missed pickup: file a complaint through the ward office within 24 hours, mentioning your zone number.
- Bulk waste (construction debris, furniture) is not collected by regular trucks; book a paid special pickup.
- Burning garbage in public areas is prohibited and attracts a fine.
